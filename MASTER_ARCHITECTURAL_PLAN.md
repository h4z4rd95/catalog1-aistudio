## 3. Audio Architecture Overhaul (Zero Noise Generative Engine)

### Problem Identified
Previous ambient sound relied on static continuous multi-frequency sine drone stacks which created harsh acoustic build-up perceived as noise.

### Solution Implemented
Re-architected the `AudioEngine` in `/src/utils/audio.ts` into a **Generative Crystal Bell & Ambient Pad Engine**:
- **Acoustic Sub-Floor**: Pure C2 65.4Hz sub-sine filtered through a strict 160Hz lowpass filter with imperceptible gain (0.008) for warm acoustic room depth.
- **Generative Bell Drops**: Procedural timer randomly picking notes from pentatonic musical scales with 3.8s exponential fade-out (ADSR: 0.35s attack, 3.8s decay, zero clipping).
- **Scale Presets**:
  - `SANCTUARY`: Eb Major Pentatonic crystal chimes
  - `SOLFEGGIO_528`: Sacred Solfeggio meditative frequencies
  - `ZEN_WARMTH`: A-Minor singing bowl harmonics
  - `CELESTIAL`: F# Lydian starry bells
- **Instant Clean Disconnect**: Zero residual noise on mute or preset switch.
